Started by an apprentice from England. Still run by his family.

Don Letts trained as a plumber's apprentice in England for five years and ran his own company there before moving to California in 1966. He worked for other outfits for years before his grandson Shane showed real ability for the trade. That's when they started Letts Plumbing together.
